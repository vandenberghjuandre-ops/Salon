GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked.

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 8 weeks out. Popular stylists and Saturday slots go quickly, so members get first access.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes — message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your slot and we'll move it, no charge.

How does the membership billing work?

Memberships renew monthly. You can pause or cancel any time by messaging the salon directly.

Do you take walk-ins?

Limited walk-in slots are available Tuesday–Thursday. Booking ahead guarantees your stylist and time.
